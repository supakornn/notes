> [Make Spotify yours.](https://spicetify.app/) Spicetify

Spicetify is a tool that allows you to fully customize Spotify’s interface and features, from colors and themes to adding extensions that enhance functionality. It empowers users to create a personalized listening experience tailored to their preferences, going beyond the default Spotify setup. In short, Spicetify lets you make Spotify truly your own.
