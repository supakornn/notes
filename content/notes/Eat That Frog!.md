Book by **Brian Tracy**.

> “The frog” is the hardest, most important task. Do it first.

My short notes on the 21 rules:

1. **Set the table:** Be clear about the goal.
2. **Plan every day in advance:** Decide before the day starts.
3. **Apply the 80/20 rule:** Find the work with the biggest effect.
4. **Consider the consequences:** Prefer work with long-term value.
5. **Practice creative procrastination:** Deliberately leave low-value work undone.
6. **Use the ABCDE method:** Do A tasks before lower-priority tasks.
7. **Focus on key result areas:** Know what your role is responsible for.
8. **Apply the Law of Three:** Identify the three tasks that matter most.
9. **Prepare before you begin:** Remove easy excuses to delay.
10. **Take it one oil barrel at a time:** Break a large task into the next small action.
11. **Upgrade key skills:** Get better at the work that matters.
12. **Identify key constraints:** Find the bottleneck first.
13. **Put pressure on yourself:** Set your own deadline when nobody else has.
14. **Maximize personal powers:** Sleep, health, and energy affect output.
15. **Motivate yourself into action:** Focus on the next step.
16. **Practice creative procrastination with technology:** Turn off distractions.
17. **Do the most difficult task first:** Use your best hours well.
18. **Slice and dice the task:** Start with a small piece.
19. **Create large chunks of time:** Protect uninterrupted time for deep work.
20. **Develop a sense of urgency:** Start instead of waiting for the perfect moment.
21. **Single-handle every task:** Stay with an important task until it is done.
