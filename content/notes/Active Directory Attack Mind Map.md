**High-level categories:**
- **Discovery & Recon:** LDAP, Kerberos, SMB enumeration
- **Credential Theft / Abuse:** Hashes, tickets, cracking
- **Privilege Escalation:** ACL abuse, Kerberos delegation, misconfig
- **Lateral Movement:** Pass-the-hash, Pass-the-ticket, relay attacks
- **Persistence:** AD object backdoors, GPO abuse, certificate persistence
- **Advanced Vectors:** SCCM, domain trusts, pivoting to critical hosts
