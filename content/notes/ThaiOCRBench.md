title: ThaiOCRBench
---
[source ](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.04479)

ThaiOCRBench is a new benchmark designed to evaluate vision-language models (VLMs) on Thai text in images. It includes 2,808 human-annotated samples across 13 task types, focusing on document understanding. Testing shows that proprietary models outperform open-source ones, especially in challenging tasks like fine-grained text recognition and handwriting. The benchmark highlights key issues such as language bias, structural errors, and hallucinations, and provides a standardized way to improve Thai-language visual understanding.
