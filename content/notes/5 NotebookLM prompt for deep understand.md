### Prompt 1: 

"What are the 5 core mental models that every expert in this field shares and how does each one change the way they see problems?"

### Prompt 2:

"Show me the 3 most fundamental disagreements between experts in this field and give me the strongest argument from each side."

### Prompt 3:

"Generate 10 questions that would immediately reveal whether someone deeply understands this subject or just memorized surface level facts."

### Prompt 4:

"Explain exactly why this answer is wrong, what gap in my understanding it reveals and where in the source material I can fix it."

### Prompt 5:

"What are the 5 questions my professor or thesis advisor is most likely to ask me and what does a truly impressive answer to each one look like?"
