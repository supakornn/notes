Outside of work and university, I tend to move around different areas in tech. Sometimes AI, sometimes systems programming, sometimes cybersecurity. I also spend time doing [[CTF| CTFs]] — mostly because I enjoy the problem-solving mindset and the way it forces you to think differently.
