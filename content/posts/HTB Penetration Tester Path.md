I have always liked computer science, and movies made hacking look cool. At first, I was basically a script kiddie: trying things without understanding how they worked. I just wanted to do something that looked cool.

But one day, I realized I needed to grow beyond being just a kid who breaks things. I wanted to truly understand the world of hacking and what happens behind the scenes. So, I started learning hacking by following the **Penetration Tester Path** from [Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com). I studied everything in the path, including all the hands-on labs, theory, and practical exercises.

![[photo-output (1).jpg | 800]]

It took me about half a year to complete. I mostly studied after school because I am a university student, and I dedicated almost all of my free time to it. The path covered a lot of theory and hands-on work. I spent around four to five hours a day studying and practicing.

![[Pasted image 20260509134942.png | 800]]

I completed the [Penetration Tester Path](https://academy.hackthebox.com/path/preview/penetration-tester). It gave me a stronger foundation and a better picture of how systems and networks connect. I do not plan to pursue cybersecurity professionally. I explore [[ctfs|CTFs]] and security challenges as a hobby because I enjoy figuring out how things work.
