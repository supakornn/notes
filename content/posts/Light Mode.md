If I told people in the developer community that I use light mode, most of them would probably think I’m crazy. However, after trying it for a month, I’ve noticed a significant change in my productivity. I’d like to share the benefits I experienced and explain why I decided to switch from dark mode after using it for most of my life.

First of all, I should mention that I used to strongly dislike light mode because it felt harsh on my eyes. I had been using dark mode since I got my first computer—almost 10 years ago. So why did I suddenly decide to switch to light mode for everything (IDE, browser, and any application that supports it)?

---
## Why I decided to try light mode

It started simply because I got bored of seeing the same color scheme every day. It began to feel monotonous, and I found myself becoming less motivated to code or work. So I thought, “Maybe I should try using light mode for a day or a week, just for a change of pace.”  
Yes, it really started from that simple (and slightly silly) idea.

---
## My experience after using light mode for a month

Surprisingly, I became more productive. My mind felt more active, and I was less prone to feeling sluggish or sleepy while working. It seems that the brighter interface helped me stay more alert and engaged throughout the day.

---
## About eye health

For anyone concerned about my eye health, I took several precautions. I wear blue light–blocking glasses, use a monitor light bar, and keep a desk lamp on while working. These adjustments have been very helpful in reducing eye strain and protecting my vision.
