Book, written by **Eric Evans**

## Chapter 1 - Crunching Knowledge
- The hardest part is understanding how the business really works, not writing the code.
- Talk with domain experts to learn the real rules, terms, and problems.
- Build a simple model that helps the software make the right business decisions.
- Update the model when the team learns something new.
## Chapter 2 - Communication and the Use of Language
- Use the same business words in meetings, code, tests, and documents.
- Make sure everyone means the same thing when they use an important word.
- Talk through examples with domain experts to find unclear or missing ideas.
- Name code clearly so it matches the words the business uses.
